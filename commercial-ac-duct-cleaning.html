Indoor Air Quality Risk

What Is Really Inside Your AC Ducts?

Abu Dhabi's dust, humidity and year-round AC use mean your ducts collect dust, mould and bacteria quietly. You rarely see it, but your staff and customers breathe it every day.

Health Risk

Poor Air Quality

Dust, spores and airborne allergens accumulate silently in the duct network and are continuously blown back into your office, clinic or classroom.

Financial Waste

Higher Energy Bills

Clogged ducts restrict airflow and force commercial chillers, AHUs and FCUs to run continuously at peak power to reach target thermostat temperatures.

System Strain

Frequent Breakdowns

Accumulated dirt and grime strain blower motors and freeze cooling coils, creating unexpected breakdowns and expensive emergency HVAC replacements.

Staff Discomfort

Unhealthy Workplace

Stale air and musty odors trigger respiratory irritation, frequent employee sick leaves, reduced daily focus, and negative visitor first impressions.

Regulatory Audit

Compliance Pressure

Healthcare facilities, education institutions, nurseries and food establishments across Abu Dhabi face stringent hygiene inspections and mandate clean air documentation.
